I am someone who is fascinated by science and technology and take it upon myself to keep in the loop with the latest developments. Artificial Intelligence (as much as I don't like the word) is one of these things.
In both my professional and personal life, I experiment and use some tools enabled and powered by these large language models. 
Here is where I detail what I use these technologies for.

AI Policy

I use artificial intelligence powered tools such as large language models and coding agents to to create, maintain, and edit the backend of this blog and website. This website is a static website built using GitHub pages and Jekyll. I may write a post in the future about the architecture of this site. If so, I will link it here. 
I sometimes use them to create new files, apply consistent formatting, check in and check out of GitHub, and assist with any troubleshooting questions or errors. 

I **do not** use LLM assistance in the writing of my content. I like to write prose with my own voice and style, and I am keen to keep this.

However, I do have some caveats.
- I run an application on my computer that use small open source transcription models to turn my voice into text.
- I may also use a terminal-based language model agent to create the boilerplate (sections, tags etc.) for a new blog post. 
- In some word processing tools, next word prediction or SPaG checknig is enabled and often these features are powered by AI. 
- I may also use models to reformat and refactor blog posts into usable HTML or markdown formats, making care not to change the actual content of the prose in the the blog post.
- I very occasionally use LLMs as a thesaurus.
