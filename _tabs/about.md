# About Me

Hi! I'm **Alex Torrens**. Welcome to my personal corner of the web. 

I set up this blog as a space to get some of my ideas, notes, recommendations, and general nerdery out into the open, rather than letting them gather digital dust in my cloud storage!

It's a place for me to share stuff that I don't mind people seeing, and to document my learning process as I dive into new tech and computer concepts.

---

### My Interests & Passion Areas

I have a wide range of hobbies and curiosities. You'll likely see posts here covering:

*   **🏃‍♂️ Sport & Fitness:** Both watching and playing—races, training, gear reviews, and tracking activities (I'm a heavy Garmin and Strava user!).
*   **💻 Tech & Customization:** Homescreens, daily carries, conventions, productivity apps, and experimenting with software/hardware.
*   **🌍 Travel & Adventure:** Packing lists, detailed itineraries, and trip reports (like my recent surfing and cycling trip to Taghazout, Morocco!).
*   **🍳 Cooking:** Experimenting with new techniques, recipes, kitchen gear, and delicious plant-based cooking.
*   **📸 Photography:** I'm a total beginner learning the ropes, so I hope to share visual updates as I improve.
*   **🔬 Science & Physics:** Fascinated by general nerdery, science, and the equations that explain how the universe works.

---

### What You'll Find Here

This blog is updated slowly and organically. Some of the topics I plan to cover (or have already written about) include:

*   Detailed trip reports and packing lists.
*   What's in my bag / my daily carry.
*   My phone homescreens, apps, and "house style" conventions.
*   Kit recommendations for cooking, sports, and travel.
*   Pedantic rants about incredibly minor details in tech and life.
*   How I'm attempting to learn new languages.
*   No-AI content (I use AI for coding and technical help, but **never** for writing these posts!).

*Thanks for stopping by and reading!*
